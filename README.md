# Tata Group Job Simulation — Forage

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains my work and deliverables from the **Tata Group Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights Job Simulation** hosted on **Forage**.

The simulation provided an opportunity to work through realistic business scenarios and apply data analysis and visualisation techniques to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🏢 Program

* **Company:** Tata Group
* **Platform:** Forage
* **Program:** Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights
* **Focus Areas:** Data Analysis, Data Visualisation, Business Insights, and Presentation

## 🎯 Objectives

The simulation focused on:

* Analysing business data to identify meaningful patterns and trends
* Cleaning and preparing data for analysis
* Creating effective data visualisations
* Translating analytical findings into actionable business insights
* Presenting findings clearly to business stakeholders

## 📊 Key Activities

### Task 1 — Data Preparation & Analysis

Worked with the provided business dataset to understand its structure, identify relevant variables, and prepare the data for further analysis.

### Task 2 — Data Visualisation

Created visualisations to communicate important patterns, trends, and relationships within the data.

The visualisations were designed with a focus on:

* Clarity
* Relevance
* Appropriate chart selection
* Easy interpretation
* Business-focused storytelling

### Task 3 — Business Insights

Interpreted the results of the analysis and identified insights that could help stakeholders better understand the business situation and make informed decisions.

### Task 4 — Presentation

Prepared a concise presentation of the findings, focusing on communicating analytical results to a non-technical business audience.

## 💡 Key Learnings

Through this simulation, I gained practical experience in:

* Turning raw data into meaningful business insights
* Selecting appropriate visualisations for different types of information
* Communicating data-driven findings effectively
* Thinking from a business stakeholder's perspective
* Presenting analytical results in a clear and structured manner

## 📈 Outcome

The simulation strengthened my understanding of how **data analysis and visualisation can be used to support business decision-making**.

It also provided practical exposure to the process of moving from raw business data → analysis → visualisation → insights → stakeholder communication.
